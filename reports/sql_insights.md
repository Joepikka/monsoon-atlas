# SQL insights report (T224)

Ten questions answered with DuckDB SQL over the data we hold. Every table below is regenerated by `.venv/Scripts/python -m atlas.analysis.sql_insights`; `inputs: local` queries need `data/raw` (not in git), `inputs: committed` ones run in CI. Counts are exact; nothing is imputed. The S08 IMD grid appears only as an index (grid / gauge ratio), never as millimetres, and H11 item 3 still decides its publication (D105, D129). Registered crime cases are not crime incidence. S32 results (Q08, Q09) derive from KSEB pages under CC BY-SA 2.5 India: credit KSEB, share alike. Substitutions for the spec's example questions: D131.

## Q05. Does the M05 drought outlook beat the strongest simple baseline, and is the gain clearly above zero?

- **Source:** M05 registry records models/registry/M05/v1.evaluation.json and v1.monitor-2025.json (inputs derived from S08, accuracy figures only)
- **Unit:** mean absolute error of the SPI-3 forecast (SPI units, lower is better); improvement is baseline minus ridge
- **Range:** locked test 2020-2024 (60 months) and the 2025 hindcast monitor (12 months)
- **Denominator:** 1800 district-months (30 districts x 60 months) in the test, 360 (30 x 12) in 2025
- **Caveat:** Hindcast, because IMD publishes the grid yearly (D39); the baseline is persistence; the 95 percent bounds come from a paired block bootstrap over months; 2025 IMD data is provisional.

Query: `sql/insights/q05_drought_model_vs_baseline.sql`

| period | district_months | mae_baseline | mae_ridge | improvement | lower95 | upper95 | clearly_above_zero |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2020-2024 locked test | 1800 | 0.808 | 0.732 | 0.076 | 0.003 | 0.15 | True |
| 2025 hindcast monitor | 360 | 0.744 | 0.625 | 0.119 | 0.019 | 0.226 | True |
