# M21 Karnataka monthly district rain-anomaly forecast: locked test (T284)

**Status: research_only.** The named candidate (LightGBM): lower 95% bound of the MAE improvement over climatology is -0.061, not above 0; published research-only with its scores. 80% intervals covered 0.754 of test outcomes, inside the 75-85% gate, so they may be shown.

**This is a hindcast** (D39): features assume IMD rain up to the previous month was known on day 1 of the month; IMD publishes the gridded files yearly. 2025 is provisional (D98). Not a forecast service and not an emergency product.

## Question, data and target

Will next month be wetter or drier than normal in this district? Target: the z-score of month t district rain against the 1981-2010 mean and sd of that district and calendar month (S08 IMD gridded rain, 30 geoBoundaries 2021 districts; only indices leave the pipeline). Dry district-months (1981-2010 sd below 10 mm, 61 of 360) are left out, never scored. Features: anomaly lags 1-12, SPI-1/3/6 and the state-mean anomaly at t-1, calendar month. Splits: train 1982-01 to 2014-11, tune 2015-2017, calibrate 2018-2019, locked test 2020-2025 (31-day purge).

## The story before the test (train and tune only)

- Baselines (T280, D214): climatology (anomaly 0) beat persistence and last year on tune (MAE 0.907, 1.114, 1.351).
- Classical (T281, D216), tune MAE: SARIMA per district 0.911, ridge 0.914, LightGBM 0.878 (+0.029 [-0.029, +0.086] over climatology).
- Networks (T283, D217), five seeds, tune MAE mean: MLP 0.902, GCN-GRU 0.907, identity ablation 0.891; the graph added nothing (-0.015 [-0.033, +0.002]).
- Spatial (T282, D218): neighbour lags at t-1, tune MAE 0.870 vs 0.878 without; leaky cross-validation overstated skill by about 0.08 MAE; residuals are spatially correlated within a month (about 2 independent values per month), so every interval resamples whole months.
- Candidate named before the test by a written rule: **LightGBM** (lowest classical tune MAE; no network beat it with a lower 95% bound above 0; tune MAE 0.878, lower 95% bound vs climatology -0.029, so expected research-only.)

## Protocol fixed before the test was opened

Written to the gate at 2026-11-18T06:28:02.172347+00:00; the test was opened at 2026-11-18T06:28:12.258126+00:00 (git f7d075e08812, the commit before T284).

- Refit: Fixed before the test was opened (T284). Every model, the candidate and each comparison, keeps the configuration frozen on train and tune (LightGBM parameters and rounds; ridge alpha; SARIMA order per district by AIC on train; network sizes and each seed's early-stopped epoch count) and is refitted once on train + tune, target months 1982-01 to 2017-11 (31-day purge before calibrate). Nothing is re-tuned. Each refit's calibrate (2018-2019) residuals give its probabilistic ensemble (RPS, CRPS) and the candidate's interval offsets. The locked test 2020-2025 is scored once. (10740 rows.)
- Intervals: 80% nominal, D97 style. Methods: pooled split conformal (forecast plus the 10th and 90th percentiles of calibration residuals, y - forecast) and seasonal (the same, separately for June-September and the rest of the year). Chosen before the test on pre-test years only: offsets from the tune (2015-2017) residuals of the train fit, coverage checked on calibrate (2018-2019) with the same fit; the method whose coverage is closest to 80% wins (ties: narrower mean width). The chosen method's offsets are then taken from the calibrate residuals of the train + tune refit and applied unchanged to the test. Intervals are shown only if test coverage is within 75-85%.
- Promotion: candidate_eligible only if the lower 95% bound of the MAE improvement over the strongest simple baseline on the test rows (climatology, persistence or last year; paired block bootstrap by month, 1,000 resamples, seed 42) is above 0, and then served only after owner approval (D38); otherwise research_only, published with its scores.

## Limits

- Hindcast only (S08 is released yearly); 2025 is provisional (D98). M05 was tested on 2020-2024 with related inputs, so every M21 choice was fixed on train and tune only.
- 72 test months; districts move together within a month, so the effective sample is far smaller than the row count.
- One-month rain anomalies from local history alone are close to unpredictable; no ocean index (ENSO, IOD) is used (it would need a new source and verify task).
- Comparisons were scored at the same single opening and are labelled; none can be promoted from this test.
- Ballari includes Vijayanagara (geoBoundaries 2021 lines).
