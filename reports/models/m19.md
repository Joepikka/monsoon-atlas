## Planned target (unverified, never trained)

The M02 level target (reservoir x daily issue, metres) as daily q10/q50/q90 at 7 and 30 days from
one small GRU (N3: a 60-day window of 8 channels, ordered quantiles, future rain excluded), compared
with M02's strongest trained non-neural baseline on identical dates and features. The gate needs at
least 5 reservoirs with 5 years of daily data and a free GPU session (2 GPU hours per experiment at
most). Promotion needs MAE at most 0.95x M02 with a positive 95% paired skill interval, CRPS no
worse and 80% coverage of 75 to 85%. The prospective study collects from 17 Jan 2028 (train to 28
Mar, tune to 12 Apr, calibrate to 26 Apr, locked test 27 Apr to 26 May 2028), the same dates as M02.
These dates are planned only: they assume approval on schedule, the whole manifest shifts if
approval is late, and no earlier observation is invented. The card's 2017 to 2024 retrospective
splits are unreachable.
