# M16 — Cross-state sensor and market anomalies

**Status: research-only. Rule implemented; flag counts reported; precision not measured (0 of 100
adjudicated).** No quality claim is made, no flag is published as an alert, and the registry holds
no baseline score, candidate score, improvement, weights or prediction row.

An anomaly flag is a data-quality signal, not a real-world incident. A true flood looks like an
outlier too, and a flagged value is never deleted for being unusual.

## What was built (T214 to T216)

- **Rule** ([`src/atlas/features/m16.py`](../../src/atlas/features/m16.py), D157): robust z against
  the median and MAD of the 28 days before the report (a future row cannot change an earlier flag;
  mutation-tested); flag when `abs(z) > 4` or a value breaks a stated unit bound. A missing value
  never becomes 0 and never flags. A MAD of 0 gives no z: the row is counted as `mad_zero`, not
  flagged and not called normal. Fewer than 21 usable days in the window gives no z. The card's
  season/hour correction is not applied (the trailing window follows the local level; daily data
  has no hour); season only stratifies the counts below.
- **Counts** ([`m16-flag-counts.json`](m16-flag-counts.json), D158): the rule run on the held S08
  daily grids, 1981 to 2025, for the Karnataka and Kerala cell boxes. Counts only: the S08 licence
  is restricted (D129), so no rainfall value, cell or date is published. Re-run by
  `atlas.models.m16.s08_flag_counts`; a test reproduces 2025 when the local grids exist.
- **Not run:** the IsolationForest challenger (with 0 adjudicated labels no challenger can be
  evaluated) and the seasonal robust-z baseline (nothing to compare it with). The range check is
  part of the rule: 0 values below 0 mm exist in the held grids.
- **Machine-readable gate and status:** [`m16-data-gate.json`](m16-data-gate.json) and
  [`models/registry/m16.json`](../../models/registry/m16.json) carry the evidence hashes. The
  status is `research_only`, the status the release framework gives when scores are missing (D38).

## What the counts do and do not say

- Flags are 18.8% of the evaluated Karnataka cell-days, almost all above the median, and most
  fall in the monsoon. Daily rain is zero-heavy and heavy-tailed, so many of these are probably
  ordinary heavy-rain days rather than data errors. That is an observation about the rate, not a
  label: nobody has adjudicated any flag.
- 75% of Karnataka cell-days (60% in the Kerala box) have no z, mostly dry spells with a MAD of 0.
  The rule is blind there: a large rain day after a dry spell is not flagged by this rule.
- The card's target of at most 1 false alert per entity-week cannot be checked without labels.
  The flag rate on evaluated cell-days is already above 1 per entity-week, so the rule as specified
  may not meet it on rain.
- Precision (target 0.80 among 100 adjudicated flags), recall on independently known anomalies and
  the Wilson and day-block intervals are **not measured**. The two adjudication batches (T212,
  T213) are recorded as blocked in [`eval/anomalies/blocked.json`](../../eval/anomalies/blocked.json)
  (D159): the agent does not adjudicate its own detector's flags, S08 is the only feed with
  flags, and no independent evidence packet exists.

## Failure modes

True floods and monsoon bursts look like outliers: no value is deleted for being anomalous.
Missing or revised readings can trigger false alerts in a live feed; here a missing day is left
out of the window and never flags. The held grids have 0 missing days in either box (the one known gap,
12 Oct 2023, lies outside them, D40). The held S08 year files are a hindcast archive (every file was fetched in 2026); this is
not a live feed.

## Retry and retraining

Retry when the owner adjudicates 100 real flags (50 per batch, 10% second-reviewed, independent
evidence attached per flag), or when a second feed with a daily history exists. Until then M16
stays research-only. A changed target definition creates a new model version and an untouched test.
Monitor target coverage (share of cell-days with a z), slice counts by season and any stale
source; a stale source or a label revision quarantines the model.
