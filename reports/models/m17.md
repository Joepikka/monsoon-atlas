## Planned target (unverified, never trained)

A 256x256 tile at 10 m: the water or flood mask at acquisition time, not a future flood prediction.
Labels would be independent event inundation polygons that exclude permanent water, never masks made
from the same SAR threshold. Inputs would be calibrated Sentinel-1 VV and VH log backscatter plus
the VV difference. Baselines: a training-fitted VV change threshold and a logistic pixel classifier;
candidate: a small U-Net. The gate needs at least 5 distinct events with independent masks and a
verified S42 and S43 licence. Plan card splits: train 2015 to 2017 events, tune the 2018 Kerala
event only if a separate later test exists, test 2019 to 2020 events, with spatially disjoint
events; none is reachable.
