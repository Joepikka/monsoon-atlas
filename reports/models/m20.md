## Planned target (unverified, never trained)

For a chosen place and issue time, recompute the already-approved flood score under 0.5x, 1.0x and
1.5x observed input rain and show the change against the original. The rain feature group scales
coherently, non-rain features and the label time stay frozen, and the range is limited to the
training 1st to 99th percentile. This is model sensitivity, not a causal intervention or flood
depth. Baselines: the original prediction at factor 1.0 and the no-change output; the factor 1.0
output must equal the base within 1e-6, and extrapolation is rejected. There is no new learner and
no new split: the explorer inherits the approved base model's test and uncertainty. Non-monotonic
effects would be shown, not edited away.
