## Planned target (unverified, never trained)

Market-commodity weekly log modal price response over weeks 0..4 after local rain exceeds the training calendar-week 95th percentile. The estimand is a conditional association unless identification assumptions hold; a Granger test is not proof of causation. The gate needs at least 30 markets and 50 independent heavy-rain events, with pre-trends, overlap and spillovers audited. Plan card splits: train 2021-2023, tune H1 2024, calibrate H2 2024, locked test 2025-01-01 to 2026-05-31, purge 28 days; none is reachable.
