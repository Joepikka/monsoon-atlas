## Planned target (unverified, never trained)

The same 24 h flood-event definition, spatial aggregation and surveillance completeness as M01 and
M08, scored for Kerala with a frozen Karnataka model (zero-shot) and with small-sample
recalibration. Baselines: Kerala prevalence, Kerala logistic and the frozen Karnataka score.
Candidate: the frozen LightGBM with intercept or isotonic recalibration fitted on Kerala validation
rows only. Features are the intersection of the M01 and M08 features available at issue time,
without state-specific dam fields. Plan card splits: Karnataka model frozen through 2022, Kerala
tune and calibrate 2023, test 2024, separate from the 2018 replay; none are reachable. The card's
alternative is a prospective 2027 monsoon set. Negative transfer would be a publishable result, but
only once labels exist.
