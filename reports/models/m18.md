## Planned target (unverified, never trained)

For a permitted article: flood, landslide, fire, transport or other, plus the event date and the
public place mentioned. Labels are manually annotated; any pin is preliminary and human-reviewed;
no personal entity is extracted, and a news allegation is not a verified incident. Baselines:
keyword rules with the gazetteer, and a TF-IDF logistic classifier. Candidate: frozen
multilingual-e5 embeddings with a logistic classifier first; a MuRIL classifier only after a
licence and model compatibility gate. No embedding model was downloaded and no deep-learning
package was installed. Release targets: event macro-F1 at least baseline plus 0.05, place
precision 0.90, event date exact match 0.90, pin precision 0.95 after review, with a
document-block 95% interval. The prospective study collects from 18 Oct 2027 (train to 16 Dec,
tune to 15 Jan, calibrate to 30 Jan, locked test 31 Jan to 14 Feb 2028). These dates are planned
only: they assume approval on schedule, the whole manifest shifts if approval is late, and no
earlier observation is invented. The evaluation is publisher-held-out and temporal, with
syndicated duplicates removed and no training translations of test articles.
