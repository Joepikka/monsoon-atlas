## Adapter (T070)

`src/atlas/ingest/s19.py` fetches the published tile index, resolves each requested cell's URL
from it (default `3bb`), and streams the shard straight to the raw store with the new
`Fetcher.fetch_large` / `RawStore.put_stream` (no copy in memory, byte budget enforced, no
retry). `parse_batches(raw, config, box=...)` reads the gzipped CSV row by row, keeps buildings
whose centroid lies in the box, drops repeated Plus Codes within the release, rejects changed
columns, short rows, confidence outside 0.65..1 and non-positive areas, converts WKT to WKB
(shapely, geo lock) and yields tables of at most 200,000 rows; `parse` concatenates them for
small inputs. Columns: `building_id`, `source_release`, `geometry`, `area_m2`, `confidence`,
`plus_code` (`schemas/s19.json`); provenance `model`. The fixture is the first 300 rows of cell
3b3 (CC BY 4.0). The state run (4.8 GB download, then clipping) is left to the feature task
that needs buildings (F020, T105). S19 is enabled once per release.
