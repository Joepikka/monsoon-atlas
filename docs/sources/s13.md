## Adapter (T066)

`src/atlas/ingest/s13.py` fetches the EPSG:3857 capabilities, lists the newest dates in the
NOAA-20 layer's daily Time ranges, and probes the state's zoom-6 tile for up to three of them,
newest first, until one is not blank (every pixel at grey level 8 or less counts as blank).
`parse(capabilities, config, tile=probe)` checks again that the probed date is listed and not
blank, then writes one row: the envelope plus `layer_id`, `imagery_date`, `tile_template`,
`bounds` and `crs` (`schemas/s13.json`). The map shows that date. Tiles themselves stay with
GIBS. Committed fixtures (NASA open data): the NOAA-20 layer block of the real capabilities and
the 8 and 9 November tiles. The blank check needs pillow (geo lock), so its tests skip in CI.
S13 is enabled in `configs/states/ka.yaml` for a daily date check.
