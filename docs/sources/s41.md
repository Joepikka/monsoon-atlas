## Fields (Mathrubhumi, e02)

`guid` (equals the article link), `title`, `link`, `category` (site section, for example
"Kerala"), `description` (one-line summary), `pubDate` (RFC 822 with +0530 offset), and a media
thumbnail. There is no language, place or event type. Map: `guid` to `article_id`, `pubDate` to
`published_at` (convert to UTC), `title`, `link` to `canonical_url`; `publisher` comes from
configuration; `language`, `place_id`, `event_type` and `confidence` stay null (M18 outputs).

## Quota and history

No quota is published; the feed sends `Cache-Control: max-age=120` and robots.txt has no
Crawl-delay (e01, e02). The feed holds the newest 20 items (8 to 9 Nov 2026), so there is no
archive for a retrospective labelled set.

## Source-specific check: fail

The plan requires no full-text republication, no victims' names, the original publication date and
a preliminary marker. The feed supplies the original date, but without permission S41 cannot feed
M18 (training or evaluating an extractor needs consent under the Mathrubhumi terms) or the F017
event pins.

## Retry condition

Retry when the owner obtains written permission from one publisher to store headline, link and
timestamp metadata from its RSS feed for a non-commercial public map and a classifier, or a
publisher issues an open licence. The owner records the permission text and permitted feed URLs;
then re-verify fields, quota and history and enable only those feeds. Dependents T126 and T211
stay disabled until then.
