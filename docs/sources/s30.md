## Plan checks (pass)

- **Calendar exceptions.** BMRCL: services weekday (Tue-Sat), monday, sunday and holiday, with 64
  calendar_dates rows (32 holiday additions, 32 removals). BMTC: one service every day and no
  exceptions, so BMTC holiday timetables are unknown.
- **Times above 24:00.** BMRCL: 150 arrivals at or after 24:00 in 23 trips, latest departure
  24:29:25. BMTC: none (latest 23:59:00).
- **Stop coordinates.** All present. BMRCL stops lie in 12.82-13.06 N, 77.46-77.76 E. 163 BMTC
  stops are more than 100 km from Bengaluru (KSRTC bus stands up to 17.77 N); kept as published.
- **Not live GPS.** The BMTC README says timetables are known to be inaccurate and only routes
  with live tracking are included; BMRCL intermediate times are approximated from spacing,
  dwell and speed (`timepoint` 0). Every S30 position is a scheduled estimate.
