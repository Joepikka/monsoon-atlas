## Decoding and sea-level check (pass)

Terrarium: `metres = R * 256 + G + B / 256 - 32768` (e03).

| Tile | Decoded range | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Bengaluru, z10/732/474 | 767 to 960 m, centre 879 m | e05 |
| Arabian Sea off Karnataka, z8/180/119 | down to -2,488 m (sea floor), coast pixels slightly above 0 | e06 |

Rules for T068 and the map:

1. Decode with the formula above; never treat 0 as missing.
2. Values below 0 over the sea are bathymetry, not land below sea level; hillshade and
   elevation readouts over the sea say "sea".
3. Tiles stay on S3; only tile metadata is stored.

## Adapter (T068)

`src/atlas/ingest/s14.py` fetches requested `terrarium/{z}/{x}/{y}.png` tiles on demand, decodes
every pixel with the Terrarium formula, rejects tiles that are not 256 x 256 RGB PNGs or that
decode outside the documented -11000 to 8900 m, and writes one row per tile: the envelope plus
`tile_key`, `z`, `x`, `y`, `elevation_encoding`, `crs`, `min_m` and `max_m`
(`schemas/s14.json`). Sea tiles keep their negative bathymetry. Provenance is `model` (gridded
elevation models). The Bengaluru and Arabian Sea tiles (e05, e06) are committed fixtures;
decoding needs pillow (geo lock), so the tests skip in CI. S14 is enabled on demand.
