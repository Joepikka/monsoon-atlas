## Adapter (T074)

`src/atlas/ingest/s21.py` fetches stations.json and streams schedules.json, both at the pinned
commit, and refuses files from any other commit. It keeps stops at stations whose published
state is the configured state and writes the envelope plus `station_id`, `train_id`,
`stop_sequence` (file order), `arrival_text`, `departure_text`, `journey_day`, `lat` and `lon`
(`schemas/s21.json`, D111). "None" becomes null; stops of trains whose ids do not rise are flagged
`imputed`; changed columns, malformed times, a journey day below 1 or an unknown station fail the
file. Provenance is `reconstructed` and `published_at` is the commit time. Fixtures are real
CC0 subsets: three Karnataka trains, one other train and their stations. S21 is enabled.
