# S37 2018 landslide inventory / ISRO Atlas: discovery (T123)

**Outcome: blocked.** NRSC publishes no downloadable per-landslide inventory. The one file it
links is the Landslide Atlas of India PDF, which has maps as figures and landslide counts per
state and year. Checked on 9 Nov 2026 (8 evidence files, fetched 15:30 to 15:38 UTC, 3 seconds
apart). Structured record: [`configs/sources/s37.yaml`](../../configs/sources/s37.yaml). Evidence
manifest: [`tests/fixtures/s37/manifest.json`](../../tests/fixtures/s37/manifest.json).

## Access

- www.nrsc.gov.in redirects to `/nrscnew/`; its menu links are relative to that path (e01).
  Resources > Atlas lists ten atlases (e02); the Landslide Atlas page (e05) links one PDF:
  `Landslides_Atlas_of_India_Updated_25Aug2023.pdf` (e07, 94 pages, 29 MB, no account or key).
- The Atlas says the inventory (about 80,000 landslides, 1998 to 2022; seasonal 2014 and 2017,
  event-based, route-wise) is "available in a web GIS platform in the Bhuvan portal" (page 19).
  The Bhuvan page that NRSC links is a client-rendered app shell with no download link or data
  service description (e08). No endpoint was guessed and no shapefile, GeoJSON or CSV is linked
  from any NRSC page read (e02, e03, e05).
- robots.txt returns 404 on both hosts (see `failed_fetches` in the YAML).

## Licence: restricted

Terms and Conditions (e06): copyright NRSC, ISRO; downloadable content may be used for
non-commercial purposes with "Source: NRSC, ISRO/DOS" and a link to www.nrsc.gov.in; derivative
works are allowed; all data stay NRSC property. The Atlas front matter allows educational or
non-profit reproduction with citation and forbids resale and other commercial use (e07). The PDF
stays in `data/raw` and is not redistributed.

## What the Atlas holds for Kerala

Table 2 (PDF page 34, Atlas page 19): 9 landslides mapped for the 2014 monsoon, 45 for 2017, and
event-based inventories of 5,191 (2018), 756 (2019), 9 (2020) and 29 (2021): 6,039 in all. The
Kerala map (Figure 41, 2014 to 2021) is an image. No landslide ID, coordinate, date per landslide
or positional error is printed.

## Source-specific check: fail

The plan says Atlas maps are not necessarily reusable geospatial labels and unknown occurrence
dates exclude temporal scoring. With counts only, M09 (landslide susceptibility, plan T136)
cannot meet its gate of at least 200 independent landslides with positional error of 100 m or
less and a survey footprint. T124 therefore implements only a disabled adapter. T129 audits Kerala
labels without S37; T136 stays blocked on this source.
