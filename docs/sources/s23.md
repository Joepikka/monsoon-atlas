**Data quality (93,423 distinct paged rows; rules for T271, not blockers):** 20 rows break
min <= modal <= max; 31 have a zero min or max; no modal is <= 0; modal runs 1 to 20,000 (26
below 100, for example Shimoga 2023-24 at 15 to 50, which look like per-kg entries; 39 above
10,000, 30 of them in the late-2019 onion price spike). 3 keys (date, district, market, variety,
grade) repeat with different prices. Prices and Commodity_Code are strings in older rows and
integers from Nov 2025. Names vary: 41 district spellings for about 31 districts
(Bangalore/Bengaluru, Belgaum/Belagavi, Mysore/Mysuru, Shimoga/Shivamogga, Tumkur/Tumakuru,
Kalburgi/Kalaburagi, Bijapur/Vijayapura, Chikmagalur/Chikkamagaluru and others), 164 market names
(an "APMC" suffix appears from Nov 2025) and 11 markets filed under two district names.

**Rules for the backfill (T270):** one request per Arrival_Date with `filters[State]` and
`filters[Commodity]=Onion`, limit at least the day's total; at most one request per 2 s; never
offset-page a large query. The key goes only into the request as a secret parameter; stored URLs,
the ledger and evidence carry public parameters only.

The [resource page](https://www.data.gov.in/resource/current-daily-price-various-commodities-various-markets-mandi)
and [GODL text](https://www.data.gov.in/Godl) rendered in a fresh browser without
authentication or a challenge. The earlier Akamai 403 remains a dated HTTP-client
failure, not a claim that the website is inaccessible today. A separate catalogue
navigation reached a sign-in page and was closed without interaction (D53).

GODL permits lawful reuse, including commercial use, subject to its conditions.
Any use must cite the provider, dataset, publication/version date, resource URL and
licence URL, preserve non-endorsement, and respect exclusions. The numeric
`field_license: 1` in e05 is not decoded or used as the licence proof. No
redistribution is introduced here.

Evidence e04 to e06 consists of explicitly labelled browser projections (D53),
not unmodified HTTP responses. They retain source URLs, UTC capture times,
observed HTTP 200 status, field paths/selectors and transformations. Only metadata
and licence text is retained. The content-addressed files stay local under
`data/raw/S23`; git contains only the manifest.
