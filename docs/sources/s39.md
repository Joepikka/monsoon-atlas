## Documented facts

- Requests: `https://api.telegram.org/bot<token>/METHOD_NAME` over HTTPS (e01).
- Limits: about one message per second per chat, 20 per minute in groups, about 30 per second
  for broadcasts; 429 beyond that, with `retry_after` seconds to wait (e01, e02).
- Terms: continued use of the platform means accepting the developer terms; any personal data
  needs a privacy policy (e03).

Rules once unblocked: post only to the owner's one public channel; never read chat history,
members or subscribers; honour `retry_after`; official alerts and model outputs stay labelled
separately in every message.

**Retry:** the owner creates the bot and channel (H08), accepts the terms and stores
`TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` in `.env`; then T083 runs again.
