## Checks (pass) and rules for T088

1. Counts only: no names, no case locations; nothing personal is stored.
2. Police units are not districts: six city commissionerates are separate from their districts;
   range rows are empty headings; Karnataka Railways, Coastal Security Police and CID have no area.
   Keep the published unit; map to a district only where one unit equals one district.
3. Names differ between years (Shimoga / Shivamogga, Kalaburgi / Kalaburagi, KRailways).
4. Registered cases are not crime incidence: every display says so (under-reporting).
5. Offence heads change between years and the BNS replaced the IPC from July 2024; never compare
   heads across that break.
6. Credit Karnataka State Police and OpenCity; fetch 10 seconds apart.
