## Access

- The area API is `/api/area/csv/[MAP_KEY]/[SOURCE]/[AREA_COORDINATES]/[DAY_RANGE]`, with a day
  range of 1 to 5 (e06). robots.txt allows `/api/` (e01).
- The owner's key is `NASA_FIRMS_MAP_KEY` in `.env` (H09). Its status service reported 5,000
  transactions per 10 minutes on 12 Nov 2026; the planned schedule uses about 4 a day.
- The key is part of the URL path. `KeyPathFetcher` in `src/atlas/ingest/s25.py` puts it into
  the outgoing request only; every recorded URL, raw file, ledger line and manifest keeps the
  literal segment `MAP_KEY`.

## What is known

- Licence: "NASA supports full and open sharing of data"; credit FIRMS, LANCE and ESDIS (e02).
- Latency: global data "within 3 hours of satellite observation" (e02). Each row also carries
  its measured latency (fetch time minus acquisition time).
- History: VIIRS NOAA-20 NRT covers 2026-08-01 to 2026-11-12 (e08). Older standard-processing
  (SP) data are a separate product and are not used.
- Fields (e05, e09): latitude, longitude (pixel centre), acq_date and acq_time (HHMM, UTC),
  satellite (N, N20, N21), confidence l/n/h, FRP in MW.

## Source check (plan rule, on e09)

Karnataka box, VIIRS NOAA-20 NRT, 2 days: 113 detections, all acquired on 2026-11-11, 0
duplicates on satellite, acquisition time and position; confidence 101 nominal, 8 low, 4 high;
FRP 0.35 to 16.7 MW. A hotspot is a satellite thermal anomaly, not a confirmed building fire.

## Adapter (T076)

`S25(state, fetcher).fetch(ctx, box=..., days=...)` requests one area CSV;
`parse(raw, config, area_wkb=...)` keeps detections inside the state polygon, drops repeats and
maps confidence to low, nominal or high. Columns: [`schemas/s25.json`](../../schemas/s25.json).
The test fixture `tests/fixtures/s25_firms_viirs_noaa20_ka_20261112.csv` is the real e09 CSV.
The fire context layer is available but not mounted until a public snapshot carries it (T045).
