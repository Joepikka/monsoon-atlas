## What is known

- Licence: "NASA supports full and open sharing of data"; credit FIRMS, LANCE and ESDIS (e02).
- Latency: global data "within 3 hours of satellite observation" (e02).
- VIIRS 375 m fields (e05): latitude, longitude (pixel centre), acquisition date and UTC time,
  satellite (N, N20, N21), confidence low/nominal/high, FRP in MW.
- A hotspot is a hot pixel, not a confirmed building fire; Suomi NPP (N) no longer delivers data.

## Why it is blocked

1. The area API needs a MAP_KEY; getting one is owner step H09 and it is not in `.env`.
2. `/active_fire/` and `/web-services/` are script-rendered shells (e03, e04), and robots.txt
   disallows `/dist/` (e01), so no key-free file link could be read by the scripted client. The
   browser pane was unavailable.

**Retry:** the owner gets a MAP_KEY at FIRMS (H09), stores it as `NASA_FIRMS_MAP_KEY` in `.env`,
and T075 runs again. T076 is a disabled adapter until then.
